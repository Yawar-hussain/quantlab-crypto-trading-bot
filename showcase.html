Project Journey
From Idea to Live System

7 iterations of backtesting, each building on the last — until a proven strategy was deployed live to the cloud.

01
Setup Phase
Environment & AI Tooling
Set up Windows WSL2 with Ubuntu, configured OpenClaw AI agent (Mistral, port 18789), connected Alpaca paper trading account with $100k capital.
✓ COMPLETED
02
Strategy v1
Initial Strategy — Too Restrictive
200 EMA + RSI below 35 + Bullish Engulfing candle pattern. Result: only 1 trade in an entire year. Filters were too strict — scrapped and rebuilt.
✓ COMPLETED
03
Strategy v3–v4
Relaxed Filters & Parameter Optimisation
Removed EMA filter, raised RSI threshold to 40. Tested 3 strategies simultaneously — RSI-only emerged as best. Then tested 8 RSI buy/sell combinations. Winner: RSI 45 entry / 65 exit.
✓ COMPLETED
04
Strategy v5–v6
ATR Optimisation & 10-Coin Expansion
Tested 9 ATR × Risk:Reward combinations. ATR 1.5 + RR 1.5 gave +12% return with 43.5% win rate. Applied proven settings to 10 coins — +16.37% return, all coins profitable.
✓ COMPLETED
05
Strategy v7 — Final
12-Coin Ranking & Selection
Added ADA and MATIC. Ranked all 12 coins by P&L, win rate, and trade count. Dropped XRP (only loser at -$9) and MATIC (0 trades). Top 10 selected for live deployment.
✓ COMPLETED
06
Deployment
Oracle Cloud VPS — 24/7 Live
Provisioned VM.Standard.E2.1.Micro on Oracle Cloud Free Tier, UK South London. Configured VCN, security lists, internet gateway. Deployed with Python venv + nohup for persistent uptime.
✓ LIVE NOW
07
Regime Bot — Current
3-Strategy Adaptive System
Upgraded to QUANT·LAB with 3 concurrent strategies: Trend Following (momentum/breakout), Mean Reversion (RSI/Bollinger), and Volatility Guard (VIX-based hedging overlay). Strategies activate based on market regime detection.
✓ RUNNING NOW
08
Future Work
LSTM + Sentiment Analysis
Phase 2: Train LSTM on 2+ years of hourly data to predict 4-hour price direction. Phase 3: Fine-tune BERT for crypto Twitter/Reddit sentiment. Expected win rate increase: 44% → 55%+.
